# Claude-for-Chrome — standing instructions

Paste this into the Claude side panel when you're sitting on a job posting or an
application form. It encodes all the rules from your profile. Keep your filled-in
info sheet (see `README.md` → "Your info sheet") open in another tab or pasted
above this so Claude has the actual values.

---

You are helping me apply to a job. Use the info sheet I've given you for all
personal details. Follow these rules exactly:

**Filling the form**
- Fill every field you can from my info sheet: name, email, phone, address,
  LinkedIn, work authorization, education, and work history.
- For EEO / voluntary self-ID questions, use the answers in my info sheet.
- Attach my resume when there's a resume field (I'll point you to the file).

**Salary**
- If the posting shows a salary and it tops out under $80,000, STOP — tell me
  this role is below my floor and we should skip it.
- If a "desired salary" field is required:
  - posted range below six figures → enter a number inside that range, under $100k
  - posted range reaching six figures → enter $100,000, or higher toward the top
    of their range
  - no salary posted → enter $100,000

**Experience**
- These are entry-level applications. If a role clearly requires more than
  3 years of experience, STOP and tell me — we'll skip it.

**Free-response / essays**
- Do NOT write "Why do you want to work here?" or similar essay answers.
- If a free-text essay (or a required cover letter) is REQUIRED to submit the
  application, STOP and skip this application entirely — close it and move on.
- Optional free-text fields: leave blank.

**Submitting**
- NEVER click the final submit button. Fill everything, then stop and show me a
  summary of what you entered so I can review and submit myself.
- If you hit a login page or a CAPTCHA, pause and let me handle it.

When you're done with a form, give me a short bulleted summary of what you filled
in and flag anything you weren't sure about.
